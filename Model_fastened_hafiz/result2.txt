The real bug: loops never touch the simulation

In singleday.solve(), loops_committed is used for exactly one thing:

python

n_loops = len(loops_committed) if loops_committed else 0 allowed_time_s = (...) - n_loops * (LOOP_STOP_DURATION_S + LOOP_TURNAROUND_S)

That's it. It never appears anywhere near seg_start_m, n_segments, or the route passed into forward_sim. The simulator only ever integrates Stage 1 + Stage 2 — regardless of whether the plan says 0 loop reps or 20.

Meanwhile tier3.py's DP scores each combo as dist = base_km + reps × loop_km and picks whichever combo maximizes that. Since extra reps are free in the simulation (zero real energy or time cost, only a small stop-time subtraction), the DP has every incentive to pick absurd rep counts — this is exactly why you got (14,), (20,), (22,) reps and a 5066 km total that bears no relation to what was physically simulated. It also explains the low average speeds: real driving stayed cheap and slow because the "free" loop distance was doing all the heavy lifting in the objective, so there was never pressure to actually go faster on the real portion.

This also explains your solar/motor/drain mismatch, partially. "Solar input: 6516 Wh" comes from a separate rough estimate (_estimate_solar_input_wh, full-day GHI × array specs, ignoring trailering and actual driven time) — it's not the same number that fed the real physics. "Motor energy: 2753 Wh" is real (forward_sim's integrated value). These were never meant to reconcile, and I've now fixed that by making the report prefer the real simulated solar_energy_wh when available, matching what was already done for motor energy.
